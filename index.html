Cruise Terminal
Depart 10:30am
▾
Walk out of the port and grab a petit taxi to Grand Socco. Agree on the fare before getting in — 20–30 MAD (~$2–3). The ride is about 5 minutes.
💡 Tip: Taxi drivers will approach you at the port exit. Be friendly but firm on the fare, and avoid full-day tour offers — you have your own plan!

Grand Socco
~20 minLandmark
▾
The grand gateway between modern Tangier and the ancient medina. Take in the lively roundabout, the fountain, palm trees, and the ornate gate leading into the old city. The medina entrance is just through the gate.

2
Medina Shopping — Rue Siaghine & Souks
~60–80 min totalShopping
▾
The medina's main shopping corridor. Work your way from Grand Socco inward along Rue Siaghine — most of these shops are clustered within 5 minutes of each other.
👡 Babouches (leather slippers) — ~10 min
Browse souk stalls along Rue Siaghine. Aim for 100–150 MAD (~$10–15) a pair. Feel the leather softness before buying — quality varies widely.
🧴 Argan Oil — ~15 min
Two great stops close together. Maison D'Argan Et De Miel El Kasbah (⭐4.8) — fixed prices, pure products, also stocks amlou (almond-argan paste — an excellent gift).
🌶️ Spices — ~15 min
Brasil Spices (⭐5.0) is the curated quality pick, especially for saffron. The Main Market is the full sensory experience — vendors grinding ras el hanout fresh on stone.
🪡 Rugs — Bleu de Fès — ~15 min
Tucked inside the medina. Over 2,000 handmade Berber rugs from tribes across Morocco. Knowledgeable staff explain the craftsmanship of each piece. Ships internationally.
🏺 Antiques & Pottery — ~10 min
Galerie Tindouf (⭐4.3) specializes in Moroccan and European antiques, some going back to the Roman Empire — ancient coins, pottery, furniture.

3
Petit Socco
~15 minHistoric
▾
The intimate, atmospheric heart of the old medina. This small square was once Tangier's social hub during its golden bohemian era — frequented by writers, artists, and spies. Grab a mint tea at a café and absorb the history.
Open in Google Maps ↗
4
Upper Kasbah & Alleyways
~40 minHistoric
▾
Wind uphill through the ancient kasbah's narrow whitewashed alleyways. Get gloriously lost — that's the point. The views from the top over the Strait of Gibraltar toward Spain are magnificent.
💡 Tip: Streets are steep and uneven — wear comfortable shoes. If you're in a group, agree on a meeting point in case you get separated in the alleys.

Early Afternoon — ~1:45pm
5
Café Hafa — Mint Tea
~30 minCafé
▾
A short taxi ride from the kasbah (~20 MAD). This legendary cliffside café has been open since 1921 and has hosted the Rolling Stones, the Beatles, and the Beat Generation writers. Mint tea is ~15 MAD. Incredible atmosphere.
💡 Tip: Find your own seat — service can be wonderfully relaxed. The lower terraces have the best views. Cash only.

T
Taxi — Western Cliffs Excursion
~30 min driveTaxi required
▾
Negotiate a round-trip taxi from the beach area covering both the Caves of Hercules and the Phoenician Tombs, then back to the cruise port. Agree on the total fare before departing — expect 300–400 MAD round-trip for the whole taxi.
💡 Tip: Agree the fare explicitly includes waiting time at both sites. A friendly driver who speaks some English will make this leg far more enjoyable — the front desk at your hotel can help arrange one in advance.
7
Caves of Hercules
~45 minLandmark
▾
These legendary sea caves are where Hercules rested before his labours. The famous cave opening frames a perfect natural silhouette of the African continent — one of Morocco's most iconic views. Entry is ~80 MAD. Take your time; this is the visual highlight of your day.
💡 Tip: On-site photographers offer photos for ~30 MAD. The view from outside the cave entrance is free and stunning — worth considering if you'd rather save the entry fee and maximize time at other stops.

8
Phoenician Tombs
~25 minOptional
▾
Rock-carved graves dating to 800–1000 BC, cut into the Atlantic clifftop. Free entry. The tombs themselves are raw and unofficial — but the sweeping views over the ocean and toward Spain are quietly stunning.
