Grand Socco
~20 minLandmark
▾
The grand gateway between modern Tangier and the ancient medina. Take in the lively roundabout, fountain, palm trees, and the ornate gate into the old city. Great orientation point before diving into the souks.

2
Medina Shopping — Rue Siaghine & Souks
~60–80 min totalShopping
▾
The medina's main shopping corridor. Work inward from Grand Socco — most shops are within 5 minutes of each other.
👡 Babouches — ~10 min
Souk stalls along Rue Siaghine. Aim for 100–150 MAD a pair. Feel the leather softness before buying. No-haggle option: Kasbah Collective inside the kasbah.
🧴 Argan Oil — ~15 min
Maison D'Argan Et De Miel El Kasbah (⭐4.8) — fixed prices, also stocks amlou (almond-argan paste, great edible gift). Biospartel (⭐4.9) on Rue Siaghine — black soap, rose water, argan oil.
🌶️ Spices — ~15 min
Brasil Spices (⭐5.0) for quality saffron. The Main Market for the full sensory experience — vendors grinding ras el hanout fresh. If you can only do one, do the Main Market.
🪡 Rugs — Bleu de Fès — ~15 min
2,000+ handmade Berber rugs, knowledgeable staff, rooftop terrace view over the medina. Ships internationally. Smaller rugs perfect for carry-ons.
🏺 Antiques & Pottery — ~10 min
Galerie Tindouf (⭐4.3) — antiques going back to the Roman Empire. Next door: Bazar Tindouf — ceramic tea sets, brass bracelets, decorative finds.

3
Petit Socco
~15 minHistoric
▾
The intimate heart of the old medina — once Tangier's social hub during its golden bohemian era, frequented by writers, artists, and spies. Sit at one of the old cafés and absorb the timeless atmosphere before heading up to the kasbah.

4
Upper Kasbah & Alleyways
~40 minHistoric
▾
Wind uphill through the ancient kasbah's narrow whitewashed alleyways. Get gloriously lost — the views from the top over the Strait of Gibraltar toward Spain are spectacular. Look for ornate doors and tilework as you climb. The Musée de la Kasbah (17th-century sultan's palace) is here if you'd like to pop in.
💡 Streets are steep and uneven — wear comfortable shoes. Agree on a meeting point in case your group gets separated in the alleys.

Early Afternoon — ~1:45pm
5
Café Hafa — Mint Tea
~30 minCafé
▾
Short taxi from the kasbah (~20 MAD). Legendary cliffside café open since 1921 — the Rolling Stones, the Beatles, and Beat Generation writers drank here. Terraced levels overlook the Strait of Gibraltar. On a clear day you can see Spain. Mint tea ~15 MAD.
💡 Find your own seat — service is wonderfully relaxed. Lower terraces have the best views. Cash only.

T
Taxi — Western Cliffs Excursion
~30 min driveTaxi required
▾
Negotiate a round-trip taxi covering both the Caves of Hercules and the Phoenician Tombs, then back to port. 300–400 MAD (~$30–40) round trip is fair. Ask the driver to wait at each stop.
💡 Agree the fare explicitly covers waiting time at both sites. A nearby hotel's front desk can help call a reputable driver.
7
Caves of Hercules
~45 minLandmark
▾
Legendary sea caves where Hercules rested before his labours. The cave opening frames a natural silhouette of the African continent — one of Morocco's most photographed views. Entry ~80 MAD. Surrounding cliffs offer spectacular ocean views even without entering.
💡 On-site photographers offer photos for ~30 MAD. The view from outside the entrance is free and equally stunning.

8
Phoenician Tombs
~25 minOptional
▾
Rock-carved graves dating to 800–1000 BC on the Atlantic clifftop. Free entry. Raw and unofficial, but the Atlantic views in late afternoon light are breathtaking. Skip if energy is low — the Caves will have been the highlight of this leg.
